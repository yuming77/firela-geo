What is FIREla?

FIREla is an open-source (AGPL-3.0) Beancount ecosystem for privacy-first personal finance. FIRE stands for Financial Independence, Retire Early; “la” stands for Ledger. The ecosystem ships firela-bot, a self-hostable AI financial advisor with billing import, and firela-app, a Flutter mobile client with multi-region bank statement parsers and an on-device PII sanitizer.

Data sovereignty isn't a feature — it's a foundation. Your ledger is Beancount plain text: a universal format that outlives every app.

The ecosystem

firela-bot self-hostable today

AI financial advisor with BillClaw billing import (Plaid, Gmail, GoCardless). Deploys to Cloudflare Workers' free tier. Exports Beancount, Ledger, CSV. github.com/fire-la/firela-bot

firela-app in development

Flutter mobile client with 10 registered bank statement parsers and an on-device PII sanitizer (11 detection types). Parser and sanitizer libraries are open source today. github.com/fire-la/firela-app

firela-vlt & firela-relay

Beancount ledger store and an open-banking relay where you hold the access tokens. Both in development.

Bank statement parsers (10 registered)

  • China: Alipay (mobile / web / Yu'ebao), WeChat Pay, CCB debit, CMBC credit
  • Hong Kong: HSBC credit, HSBC debit
  • Europe: Degiro

In development: Bank of China, ICBC, Deutsche Bank, Interactive Brokers.

On-device PII sanitizer (11 detection types)

Every statement is scanned and masked on your device before any data reaches a server:

  • CN: ID card, mobile phone, passport, bank card
  • HK: HKID, HK phone · US: SSN, US phone
  • EU: IBAN with checksum validation (29 European country patterns)
  • Universal: email addresses, credit card numbers

Two rule modes: regular masks personal identifiers; community masks everything for safe public sharing.

Frequently asked questions

Is FIREla free?

The code is free and open source under AGPL-3.0. firela-bot self-hosts on Cloudflare Workers' free tier at no infrastructure cost. There is no paid plan.

Is FIREla really open source?

Yes — AGPL-3.0. You can read the parser and sanitizer code, run it yourself, and verify every privacy claim on this page.

Do I need an account?

The parser and sanitizer libraries run on your device without a FIREla account. firela-bot is self-hosted on your own Cloudflare account with your own API keys.

How is FIREla different from Mint, YNAB, or Monarch?

Mint was shut down by Intuit in 2024; YNAB is closed-source at $109/year; Monarch is $99/year plus your banking credentials. FIREla gives you an open format you control instead of a subscription over your credentials.
